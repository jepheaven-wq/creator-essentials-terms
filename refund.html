Creator Essentials Studio — Digital Product Refund Policy (v1.0)

1. Digital delivery

Creator Essentials Vol. 1 is a digital product delivered after purchase. Please review the product description, included files, audio previews, and license before purchasing.

2. Technical delivery issues

If you cannot access or download your purchased files, or if a delivered file is materially corrupted or missing, contact jep.heaven@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase. We will first attempt to provide a working replacement download or missing file.

3. Refund requests

Because the product is downloadable digital content, refunds are not automatic after files have been successfully delivered or accessed. We will review refund requests individually where:

  • there is a verified technical delivery problem that cannot be resolved with replacement files;
  • duplicate billing occurred; or
  • a refund is required by applicable law.

4. Non-refundable reasons

A request is not ordinarily eligible for a refund merely because the buyer changed their mind, did not use the files, expected an unsupported format or use right, or did not review the product description/license before purchase.

5. How to request support

Email jep.heaven@gmail.com with:

  • order number;
  • purchase email address;
  • a concise description of the issue; and
  • screenshots or error details where relevant.

Do not send passwords, card numbers, or sensitive payment information.

6. Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to limit mandatory consumer rights that apply under applicable law. Lemon Squeezy, as Merchant of Record for the transaction, may also have its own refund or chargeback processes.
