Creator Essentials Studio — Buyer Privacy Notice (pemberitahuan privasi pembeli) — v1.0

1. What this notice covers

This notice describes how the seller handles personal data in connection with the digital music products sold through

the Creator Essentials Studio storefront, and how a buyer can exercise their choices. Payment, receipts, invoicing and

the checkout page itself are operated by the checkout provider acting as Merchant of Record; this notice describes what

the seller holds, and points to the provider for what the provider holds.

2. What we receive, and why

  • Order information from the provider (contract): order reference, purchase email address, product purchased,

amount and currency, country/region, and refund or dispute status. We use it to deliver the files, answer support

requests, keep an accurate sales record, and meet tax/accounting duties. We do not receive card numbers or full

payment credentials; payment data stays with the provider.

  • Support messages you send us (contract / legitimate interest): your email address, the content of your message,

and any attachment you choose to send. We use it only to resolve your issue. Please do not send passwords, card

numbers, or government identifiers.

  • Optional feedback (consent): if we offer a feedback form, the rating and comment you choose to submit. Submitting

is optional and is not required to purchase, download, or request support.

  • Provider-side technical records: access logs and download counters generated by the provider. They are held by

the provider under its own notice.

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising pixels, no cross-site tracking, and no data sold or shared for third-party marketing.
  • No profiling, no automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects for a buyer, and no

personalised pricing.

  • No account creation, loyalty programme, or newsletter signup is required to buy or download.

4. Retention and minimisation

  • Order, support and tax-relevant records: kept for 24 months, then deleted; statutory tax/accounting records may

be kept for the period applicable law requires.

  • Optional feedback: kept for up to 24 months, and deleted earlier on request; we can record it without your name

if you prefer (see section 6).

  • Support attachments containing unnecessary personal data are deleted once the issue is resolved.

5. Who has access

Access is limited to the seller (a one-person operation). Providers that help operate the store - the Merchant of

Record and the hosting/email platforms - process data on their own terms and may process it outside Indonesia under

their own safeguards. We do not otherwise disclose buyer data, except where the law requires it.

6. Your choices and rights

Email jep.heaven@gmail.com with the subject "privacy request" to ask for: a copy of the data we hold about you;

correction of inaccurate data; deletion of support or feedback data; withdrawal of consent for optional feedback;

an anonymised copy of a feedback response; or the deletion of your order email address from our records where keeping it

is not required for tax/accounting. Where mandatory local consumer or data-protection law gives you stronger rights,

this notice does not limit them. We aim to answer within 14 days; if you are not satisfied you may complain to your

local data-protection authority.

7. Security and honest limits

We minimise the data we hold, keep credentials out of documents and chat, and rely on the provider's payment security

for payment data. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we do not promise absolute security.

8. Children

The products are made for adults working on projects; they are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly

collect personal data from children.

9. Changes

If this notice changes, the version and date change with it; a material change is applied to the sales surface before

further sales, not after them.
